About the sourcing & its limits

How this list was built

  • Live web search per specialty: "best <specialty> Port St Lucie FL", "top rated <specialty> Port St Lucie FL", "<specialty> Port St Lucie FL Google reviews"
  • Cross-checked against doctor-rating platforms: Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Vitals, Healthline, WebMD, Psychology Today, Sharecare, Birdeye, rater8, Tebra, Cleveland Clinic's own provider directory, and Three Best Rated
  • A rating is labeled "Google" only when a source explicitly attributed it to Google; every other rating names its real platform
  • Ranked primarily by rating, weighted by review count so a 5.0 with 1 review doesn't outrank a 4.9 with 300 — judgment calls are noted per provider

Known gaps

  • Several directories (Healthgrades, ThreeBestRated, Zocdoc, WebMD, Vitals, rater8) were blocked to direct page fetches during research, so some figures come from search-result snippets rather than a live page read
  • Addresses, suite numbers, and phone numbers sometimes conflict between directories — flagged per provider where known, but not independently called to confirm
  • Many providers have no public star rating at all; those are shown honestly as "Not yet rated" rather than guessed

Working now

  • Search, specialty filters, minimum‑rating and sort controls
  • Side‑by‑side comparison of up to 3 providers
  • Favorites synced to your account when signed in, saved locally otherwise
  • Community "Suggest a provider" submissions, clearly marked unverified

Suggested next steps

  • Board certification, license status, insurance‑network participation, and current new‑patient status were not verified — confirm all of these directly with the provider
  • A periodic re-crawl would catch providers who have closed, moved, or changed ratings since this snapshot
  • Direct phone confirmation of the addresses flagged as conflicting between directories
